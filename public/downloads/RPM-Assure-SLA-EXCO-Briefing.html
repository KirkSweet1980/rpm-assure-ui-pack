RPM Assure

How the application works · How SLA is calculated
17 August 2026 · SYSPRO Support & AMS Rev 5.0 · Industry measures 14 Aug 2026

1. What RPM Assure is

One boardroom view of every customer we manage. It pulls live data from SYSPRO, Pulseway (remote management), Cove (cloud backup), Bitdefender (endpoint protection) and Microsoft 365.

It does not replace those products. It answers three questions: who is in cover, is the service healthy now, and are we meeting SLA.

4. How SLA is calculated — two layers (do not mix them)

Layer A = the RPM contract (did we answer and restore tickets in Business Hours).
Layer B = operational SLA (is the platform — servers, backups, EPP — actually running).
Only covered services contribute. A No Cover pillar is dropped, not treated as 100%.

Layer A — RPM contract (SYSPRO Support & AMS Rev 5.0, August 2026)

Ticket clocks in Business Hours (08:00–17:00, 8 hours per day). These are targets, not guarantees.

How we score it. For each clocked incident in the last 30 days: did we meet the acknowledge clock? did we meet the restore clock?

Contract SLA % = tickets that met the clock ÷ tickets scored

Shown on Customer SLA. If there are no clocked tickets, we do not invent a percentage.

Layer B — Operational SLA (covered services only)

Equal average of scored, covered pillars. Microsoft 365 is never included.

1. SYSPRO — start at 100, then deduct. Target: 100% controls clear (AMS health, not ticket clocks).

SignalDeduction
Collect older than 24 hours−35 (or −50 if never collected)
Each SYSPRO job error−15, then −5 per extra error (cap −40)
Each FinSight out-of-balance line−10, then −2 per extra (cap −35)

2. RMM — servers only. Workstations never score. Target: 99.9% monthly uptime.

  • Uptime: if Pulseway has 30-day offline hours:
    (43,200 − average offline minutes) / 43,200
    Otherwise: servers online ÷ servers on the latest snapshot.
  • Critical alerts: −12 points each (cap −40).
  • Coverage line: servers reporting in the last 15 minutes ÷ servers.
  • No classified servers → not scored (not 0%).

3. Cloud Backup — target 99.5% success · 24-hour RPO.

  • Success = OK jobs ÷ (OK + failed).
  • RPO = last success within 24 hours (stale devices pull the score down).
  • Restore = passed recovery tests ÷ (passed + failed), blended in at 30% when tests exist.
  • Devices mapped but no job outcome yet → not scored (never invent 70%).

4. EPP — target 98% protected.

  • Coverage = managed endpoints ÷ (managed + unmanaged).
  • Currency = product + signatures current (when the collect has those flags).
  • Open high/critical incidents: 100% if none; −20 per open incident.

5. Microsoft 365 — Secure Score / MFA / admin posture only. Not in the SLA average.

6. What EXCO can say with confidence

  1. Cover = we contracted / mapped it. No Cover = we are not pretending to measure it.
  2. Green = covered services are meeting the live bar. Amber = watch. Red = a covered service is in breach.
  3. Contract SLA = did we answer and restore tickets in Business Hours.
  4. Service SLA = is the platform (servers, backups, EPP) actually running.
  5. Numbers on screen are from today’s collect, not a monthly spreadsheet.
Likely questions
“Why is this customer the same colour on the main page and inside?” — One formula now; Cover and RAG are unified.
“Why Cloud Backup No Cover if devices exist?” — Devices must be mapped to that customer code before they count.
“Why patch counts but not KB names?” — Pulseway’s API only sends counts. Named KBs are collected on the server and posted into Assure.
