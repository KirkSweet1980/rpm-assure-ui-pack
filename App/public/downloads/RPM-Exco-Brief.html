ExCo brief — what Assure is, and how SLA is scored

One page · August 2026 · SYSPRO Support & AMS Rev 5.0

What it is

  • One estate view of every customer we manage — SYSPRO, RMM, Cloud Backup, EndPoint Protection, Microsoft 365, and tickets.
  • Live data from the customer host and from vendor APIs. We do not invent cover.
  • ExCo sees health. Operators see the same facts inside each tenant.

Cover

Cover Live rows exist for that service (or Tickets — always on).
No cover Service is not in scope. It is not scored.

Empty tickets still show Cover. The pane says “No tickets exist for this customer.”

How SLA is calculated

Estate SLA is the average of covered services only. Uncovered services are excluded.
